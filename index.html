Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know before taking the first step toward financial freedom.

Will debt settlement affect my CIBIL score?
Yes, settlement will temporarily lower your score. However, once your debts are resolved, we help you with a credit rebuilding plan so your score recovers over time — many clients see significant improvement within 12–18 months.
Is debt settlement legal in India?
Absolutely. Debt settlement is completely legal in India and is recognized by RBI guidelines. Banks and NBFCs regularly accept settlement offers, especially when the borrower is in genuine financial hardship. Our lawyers ensure every step is fully compliant.
Can creditors sue me if I join this program?
Once you enroll, our lawyers send formal legal representation notices to all your creditors. This legally restricts them from direct contact and most collection activity. If any lender does file suit, our legal team handles it entirely at no extra cost to you.
How long does the process usually take?
Most clients see their first settlement within 6–12 months. Complex cases with multiple creditors can take up to 18–24 months. We give you a realistic timeline during your free consultation based on your specific debt profile.
What if I can't afford to pay even the settlement amount?
We work around your current financial capacity. Settlement amounts are negotiated to a level you can actually afford, and in many cases can be paid in installments. Our goal is a plan that is genuinely sustainable for you — not just the bank.
